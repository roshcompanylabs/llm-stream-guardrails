What it does not do

    
Every example above was chosen because it gets caught. That is not the measurement. A detector finds the formats it was written for; it cannot find a shape nobody thought of, a credential invented last month, or a secret the model spelled out in prose. On text nobody here wrote, recall is 47% to 81% — not 100%.

    
So it is defence in depth, not a compliance control. It makes a leak less likely. It does not make one impossible, and it is not certified against GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS or anything else — those are properties of a system and its operator, never of a dependency.
